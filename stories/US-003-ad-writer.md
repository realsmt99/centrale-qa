# US-003: A seller gets an ad written for their car

As a seller, I get a ready-to-use ad in French (a title and a description) written from my car's data, so I don't have to write it myself.

Acceptance criteria:
- The ad only mentions equipment my car really has: the standard equipment of its version, plus the options I selected.
- The gearbox, fuel, power, year, mileage and price in the ad match my car.
- I can only select options that exist for my version.
- Only signed-in sellers (with a token) can generate an ad.
