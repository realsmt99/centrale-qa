# US-002: A seller gets a price estimate

As a private seller, I get a suggested price for my car from its exact version, its year and its mileage, before I write my ad.

Acceptance criteria:
- The estimate comes with a range (low and high) around it.
- An older car is never estimated higher than the same car from a newer year.
- A car with more kilometres is never estimated higher than the same car with fewer kilometres.
- The estimate is never above the car's price when new.
